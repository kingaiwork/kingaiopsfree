## English

This public repository exists to help users, buyers and partners understand the KINGAI OPS value proposition without exposing implementation details.

### The commercial purpose of this public entry

A technical operator may discover KINGAI OPS because they need to manage a server. A business buyer may discover it because infrastructure work is inconsistent. An MSP may need a repeatable operating model across many customer environments. This repository gives each audience a clear path from product discovery to the right commercial conversation.

### Entry segments

**Individual operators** — Evaluate a simpler management experience.

**Small businesses** — Reduce operational fragmentation without hiring a large infrastructure team.

**MSPs and agencies** — Standardize work across multiple environments.

**Enterprises** — Explore governance, fleet visibility and managed relationships.

**Infrastructure partners** — Consider distribution, integration or service partnerships.

### Commercial journey

**Public discovery → product use → professional need → business/fleet need → enterprise governance → managed operations / MSP → strategic partnership.**

### Why the model can scale

Infrastructure creates recurring needs. A product that becomes part of the operating rhythm can support subscriptions, support, managed services and strategic infrastructure relationships.

### What creates upgrade demand

Users may need to move beyond a basic entry when they have:

- more systems to manage;
- more people operating them;
- recurring maintenance requirements;
- stronger governance needs;
- multiple customer environments;
- a need for managed support;
- higher business impact from infrastructure failures.

### Partnership opportunities

- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- MSPs and agencies;
- edge/device platforms;
- enterprise IT organizations;
- managed-service partners;
- business-system implementers;
- strategic technology and distribution partners.

### Role in the wider portfolio

This Public Edition should route interested users toward the production product at https://ops.kingai.work/ and, where relevant, toward Security, OS, Office, Customer OS and the wider KING AI ecosystem.

### Trust principle

Commercial discovery does not require technical exposure. Public materials should explain value clearly while production implementation remains private.

### Integrity standard

No fabricated customer counts, revenue, infrastructure scale, uptime, contracts, certifications or partnerships. Future business, fleet and enterprise features remain roadmap directions until released.

## 中文

本公开仓的商业作用，是让用户、采购方和合作伙伴先理解 KINGAI OPS 的价值，而不需要看到内部技术实现。

### 不同用户怎么进入

个人运维可能因为一台服务器进入；小企业可能因为运维太碎片化进入；MSP可能因为需要统一多个客户环境进入；企业可能因为治理和 Fleet 需求进入。

### 商业路径

**公开发现 → 开始使用 → 专业需求 → 企业/Fleet → 企业治理 → 托管运维/MSP → 战略合作。**

### 为什么可以形成长期商业关系

基础设施不是一次性任务。系统持续需要维护、变更、安全和支持，因此一旦产品进入日常运维节奏，就有机会形成订阅、支持、托管服务和战略关系。

### 什么情况下会产生升级需求

机器更多、操作人员更多、周期维护更多、治理要求提高、需要管理多个客户环境、需要托管支持，或者基础设施故障对业务影响变大。

### 合作方向

云/VPS与基础设施、MSP/代理商、边缘/设备、企业 IT、托管服务、业务系统实施和战略分发。

### 可信原则

公开商业介绍不需要泄露内部实现。价值讲清楚，技术继续留在私密生产仓。

### 可信标准

不虚构客户数、收入、基础设施规模、可用率、合同、认证或合作。未来企业/Fleet能力继续标注为路线图。
